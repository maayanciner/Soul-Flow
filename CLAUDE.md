## 7. Voice and writing style

- Warm, gentle, personal and encouraging. Speaks like a caring friend, not a guru.
- **Plain, simple English.** Short sentences. Easy words.
- **No em dashes.** Use commas, colons, periods or parentheses instead.
- Shares her own experience honestly ("I lived on that mountain for years").
- Grounds claims in the Rambam, simple body science, and her own story. Never over-promises.
- Light humor is welcome, especially for taboo topics (Episode 6: "So yes… today we're talking about poop").
- Greeting in DMs: **"Hi love 💛"**. Sign-off: **"With love, Maayan"**.
- Emojis: a few soft ones (💛 🌿 ✨ 🎧 ☀️ 📜), not too many.
- Health content includes a short note: *"This is education, not medical advice."* plus "see your doctor" when symptoms are serious.

## 8. Marketing system

- **Instagram Reels** are the main growth channel. Each podcast episode gets a ~50-second reel.
- **Reel formula:** strong first-second hook → Rambam quote or simple science → personal moment → 1–2 tips people can use today → open loop answered in the episode → "Comment [KEYWORD]".
- **Comment keyword → DM** (ManyChat auto-reply or by hand). Used so far: **FLOW** (Ep 6), **JOY** (Ep 8). Use a new keyword per episode.
- DMs send people to **soulflowbymaayan.com/podcast**, where they can play the episode and get the free meditation (this grows the email list).
- Film 3 hooks (A/B/C) and test B and C as **Instagram Trial Reels**.
- Captions end with an easy question and a "send this to a friend who…" line. Max 5 hashtags.
- Track: skip rate, sends, keyword comments, Story link taps, WordPress visits to /podcast and /meditation, new email sign-ups, Spotify plays.
- Email list is kept in a Google Sheet **(to confirm)**.

## 10. How to work in this repo

- This repo holds Soul Flow content, written in Markdown.
- Reel scripts live at `reels/epNN-short-topic/reel-script.md` (e.g. `reels/ep08-happiness/reel-script.md`). Follow the structure of the existing scripts: creative idea, "why it's built this way" table, beat-by-beat script (SAY / TEXT / SHOT / EDIT), clean teleprompter version, 3 hook variations, ready-to-paste caption, pinned comment, DM reply, filming guide, edit plan, posting playbook.
- Every claim in a reel must match what Maayan actually says in the episode. If you can't hear or read the episode, list what she should double-check.
- Write to Maayan as "you".
- Ask before publishing anything to the website, sending emails, or changing anything live.
