# Replying to Google reviews for Soul Flow

Write a reply for each review Maayan shares. Before writing, read `CLAUDE.md` and her brand voice file if they exist. If something you need is missing (for example, the review text or star rating isn't clear), ask her.

## Voice

- Warm and heartfelt: calm, personal, mindful wording such as "so grateful", "such a joy", "it means so much".
- At most one gentle emoji (for example 💜 or 🙏). None on a 1–3 star reply.
- Write in the same language as the review (Hebrew review → Hebrew reply, English → English).
- Sign every reply: **– Maayan, Soul Flow** (in Hebrew: **– מעיין, Soul Flow**).

## Rules for every reply

1. **Short:** 2 to 4 sentences, not counting the signature.
2. **Thank the writer by first name**, and mention **one specific thing** they wrote (the class, the painting, the meditation, their child, and so on).
3. **Never:**
   - make up facts (events, dates, staff, what happened) that the review doesn't say;
   - promise discounts, refunds, free classes or any compensation;
   - write the customer's personal details (last name, phone, email, health info, anything private they shared).
4. If the reviewer's name is missing or is clearly not a first name (a nickname or initials), thank them without a name.

## By star rating

| Rating | What to write |
|---|---|
| **4 or 5 stars** | A warm thank-you, the specific detail, and an invitation to come back. |
| **1 to 3 stars** | Apologize without arguing or explaining away. Show we took it to heart. Invite them to continue privately with "please reach out to us" (don't name a channel). **Always mark it `⚠️ For my approval`** and never present it as ready to post. |
| **Stars only, no text** | A short thank-you (1–2 sentences). No specific detail is needed, since there's none to mention. |

## Output format

For each review:

```
**[First name] · [★ rating]**
[⚠️ For my approval, only for 1–3 stars]

[reply text]

– Maayan, Soul Flow
```

When several reviews come in at once, list the 1–3 star replies first, so Maayan sees what needs her approval.

## Examples

**5 stars, Dana:** "The paint and meditate night was magical, I never thought I could paint like that!"

> Dana, thank you so much! 💜 It was such a joy to watch you discover the artist in you at our paint and meditate night. We'd love to welcome you back for the next one.
>
> – Maayan, Soul Flow

**2 stars, Lior:** "The class started late and felt rushed."

> ⚠️ For my approval
>
> Lior, thank you for sharing this, and I'm truly sorry the class started late and felt rushed. That's not the calm experience I want anyone to have, and I've taken your words to heart. Please reach out to us, I'd really like to hear more.
>
> – Maayan, Soul Flow

**5 stars, no text, Rachel:**

> Rachel, thank you so much for the five stars! 🙏
>
> – Maayan, Soul Flow
