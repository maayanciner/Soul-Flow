- Phone at eye level, framed chest-up, with some space above your head for the text.

## My edit plan

- **Pace:** I'll remove every pause and breath, and change something on screen every 2–3 seconds (a cut, a zoom, B-roll, or text).
- **First frame:** Hook text on screen right away, with no logo or intro. Most people decide within the first 2 seconds.
- **Punch-in zooms** on key words: *awful, poop, never, surprised, FLOW*.
- **Burned-in captions:** 2–4 words at a time, bold, with key words highlighted in your brand color. They sit in Instagram's safe zone (above the bottom UI and away from the buttons on the right), since most people watch on mute.
- **Rambam quote card** on a soft parchment tone, for credibility.
- **Sound:** a warm instrumental bed kept low under your voice, a soft "pop" on the poop line, and only a few transitions. If you want trending audio, add it in the Instagram app when posting, so the licensing is handled there.
- **Ending:** podcast cover plus "Comment FLOW 🎧", cut to loop smoothly back into the hook.
- **Export:** 1080×1920, H.264, 30 fps, high bitrate, loudness normalized for Instagram.

### Rambam sources used (Mishneh Torah, Hilchot De'ot, ch. 4)
- Don't delay relieving yourself, even for a moment.
- Before eating, walk or exert yourself until your body begins to warm.
- Always try to keep the bowels soft. Whenever stool is held back or passes with difficulty, illness follows.
- His remedies when the bowels are slightly blocked: for a younger person, boiled salted vegetables, or the broth of chard or cabbage, with olive oil and salt, in the morning without bread. For an older person, honey mixed with warm water in the morning, then wait about four hours before eating.
- Whoever doesn't exercise, holds it in, or has hard bowels will have days full of pain and fading strength, even if they eat good food.
