## The creative idea: "The Mountain vs. The Hills"

The episode's strongest image is the mountain (big highs, then a steep crash) versus gentle rolling hills (small, steady joys). You're an art teacher, so instead of just *saying* it, you **draw it on camera**. That gives you:

- **An unusual first frame.** Someone holding up a drawing makes people stop and look.
- **Movement on screen,** with no fancy editing needed: drawing the crash line and flipping the page.
- **An image people remember.** "Mountain or hills?" sticks in the mind and is easy to share.
- **Your brand in one shot:** art, mindfulness and health together.

**Prep:** On **Card 1**, draw one huge, sharp, spiky mountain (leave the right side blank, because you'll draw the crash line live). On **Card 2** (or the back of Card 1), draw soft rolling hills with a little sun. Use your **sage and peach** colors so it matches your brand. Have a thick marker in hand.

## Why it's built this way

| Part | What it does |

## Filming guide

**Setup**
- Vertical 9:16, 1080p or 4K, 30 fps, phone at eye level, framed chest-up with room for the cards.
- Soft window light in front of you. Quiet room; use a lav mic if you have one.

**Delivery**
- Film **each beat as its own take**, 2–3 times, with a **2-second pause** between lines.
- Practice the card moves (tap the peak, draw the crash, flip to the hills) so they land on the right word.
- Film all three hooks (A, B, C).
- Send me the **raw clips**, untrimmed.

**B-roll** (5–10 seconds each, vertical)

- [ ] Coffee pouring / your hands around a warm mug
- [ ] Sun on your face, eyes closed

- [ ] Your phone showing soulflowbymaayan.com/podcast with the episode playing

## My edit plan

- **Pace:** remove every pause, with something new on screen every 2–3 seconds.
- **First frame:** you plus the mountain card plus the hook text, with no logo or intro.
- **Card moments emphasized:** close-up on the crash line, and a match cut on the flip to the hills.
- **Captions:** burned in, 2–4 words at a time, bold, with key words (*sick, crash, here, joy*) in **sage and peach**. They sit in Instagram's safe zone (above the bottom bar and away from the buttons on the right).
- **Sound:** soft acoustic bed, kept low under your voice (add the music in Instagram's picker when posting, so it's licensed). One "whoosh down" on the crash, a soft chime on the flip.
- **End card:** Soul Up cover, "Comment JOY 💛", **soulflowbymaayan.com**, cut to loop back to the mountain.
- **Export:** 1080×1920, H.264, 30 fps, loudness normalized for Instagram.
